# Installing PSY Accounts on an Android phone (without Google Play)

The app is a single file: **PSY-Accounts.apk** (about 1 MB). It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

1. Copy `PSY-Accounts.apk` to the phone (WhatsApp/Viber to yourself, USB cable, Google Drive, or email).
2. On the phone, open the file (from the Files / Downloads app, or tap it in the chat).
3. Android will say something like *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*. Tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Google Play Protect asks, choose **Install anyway** (the app is not on the Play Store, so Play Protect does not know it).
5. Open **PSY Accounts**.

## First things to do
1. **Settings > Company details**: check the company name and address, and enter the PAN/VAT number and VAT registration status exactly as on your certificate. The app does not invent these.
2. **Accounting > Opening balances and books start date**: choose the date you start using the app (for example 1 Shrawan 2083) before entering anything else.
3. **Products and stock**: add your products with their opening stock.
4. **Accounting > Opening balances**: enter cash, bank, receivable, payable, capital and loan balances on the start date.
5. **Backup and restore > Back up now**, then **Save a copy elsewhere**.

## Updating to a newer version
Install the newer APK the same way. Your data stays, as long as the new APK is signed with the same key (all official builds of this app are). Make a backup first anyway.

Do **not** uninstall the app to update it: uninstalling deletes all data and the backups stored inside the app.
